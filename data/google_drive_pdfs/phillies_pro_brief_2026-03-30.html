Interpreting the Regression Signals

The tables below utilize Empirical Bayes (EB) Shrinkage. In plain English: we take a player's raw performance and "shrink" it toward the league average based on their sample size. The Delta column is our skepticism meter. A high positive Delta means a player is likely playing "over their skis" and is a prime candidate for a cooling-off period.
